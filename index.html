03 — Do you actually need Premium?

Not every business needs it — here's how to tell.

Not sure yet? Start with Standard. Every Standard build can move to Premium later for the price difference — same content, same structure, just the motion and photo treatment layered on top. No rebuild required.

Who should go Premium

  • The work itself is the pitch — before/after photos and finished spaces are what sells, and scroll motion makes people actually look at them.
  • Higher-ticket, considered purchases, where a polished site signals the same care as the work itself.
  • You're competing with other slick, well-funded businesses online, not just word of mouth.
  • Customers browse and compare before they call — they're not dialing mid-emergency.

Good fit: auto detailing, high-end landscaping & hardscaping, custom home builders, boutique renovations.

Who's fine with Standard

  • The customer is searching because something's broken — they want speed and a phone number, not a design experience.
  • An older or referral-driven customer base, where extra motion can read as salesy instead of trustworthy.
  • Every animation adds load time — a real cost for someone on a bad connection mid-emergency.
  • You want it live fast and affordable, with the option to upgrade once it's already working.

Good fit: plumbing, HVAC repair, appliance repair, handyman, most cleaning services.
